RESTORE TEXTGEN LLAMA.CPP
=========================

INSTALL_INTO_TEXTGEN.bat moves your original llama_cpp_binaries\bin folder to a timestamped backup such as:

bin_BACKUP_20260813_131000

To restore it:

1. Close TextGen completely.
2. Delete or rename the new llama_cpp_binaries\bin folder.
3. Rename the timestamped bin_BACKUP_... folder back to bin.
4. Start TextGen normally.
